Our Story



In 2018, an idea developed in the founder's mind when multiple cosmetic shampoo brands were available in the market. That idea was based on creating healthy shampoos for the public—products that could be accessed and trusted by everyone. So, we built a formula based on pure natural ingredients. After that, the first bottle of shampoo was tested in a laboratory, and the results were amazing. At the start, we began selling it to locals, and many people encouraged us to continue this idea. With the passage of time, there were many problems and issues, but we kept moving forward. We focused on improving our formula with every step and never compromised on quality. Customer feedback played a major role in shaping our journey. Gradually, our trust in natural herbal care grew stronger. Today, we are proud to be among the best in making herbal shampoos. These herbals are carefully designed and do not cause any harmful side effects.
